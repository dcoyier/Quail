There is a private RRF rank constant of 60.
There is a private per-arm N floor of 10 and cap of 50.

candidate_n(top_k):
  Give back min(50, max(10, top_k * 5)).

rrf_fuse(lexical_ids, semantic_ids, top_k, rank_constant default 60):
  If top_k is below 1, give back an empty list.
  For each arm, walk ids in order, skip empties/duplicates, assign 1-based ranks,
  and add 1 / (rank_constant + rank) into a fused score map.
  Remember each id's best (lowest) arm rank for tie-breaks.
  Sort by fused score descending, then best arm rank ascending, then entry id.
  Give back at most top_k ids.
