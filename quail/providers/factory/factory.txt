EmbeddingClient is a protocol: embed_texts(texts) -> list[list[float]].

build_embedding_client(profile, providers):
  For ollama, require [providers.ollama] and build OllamaEmbedder.
  For openrouter, require [providers.openrouter], resolve api_key env ref,
  and build OpenRouterEmbedder.
  Raise ProviderError when connectivity is missing.
