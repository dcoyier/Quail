OllamaEmbedder POSTs to {base_url}/api/embed with model, input texts, and dimensions.

embed_texts(texts):
  Give back one float vector per input text.
  Raise ProviderError on HTTP/JSON/shape/dimension mismatches.
  Dimension mismatch repair_hint says to align datasets.embedding.dimensions
  and re-run quail process.
  Raise ProviderError when any embedding component is non-numeric or non-finite.
