There is a max term byte budget of 40, a max query byte budget of 64 KiB,
a max leaf budget of 1024, and a max phrase token budget of 4096.
There is a hashed-term prefix called !sha256: and a no-match sentinel called
!quail:no-match!.

LeafKind is term, phrase, prefix, or any.
Leaf remembers kind and terms.
BooleanExpression remembers required leaves and excluded leaves.
OrExpression remembers child expressions.
Expression is a Leaf, BooleanExpression, or OrExpression.

prepare_text(text):
  Split on alnum/combining runs, NFC-normalize and lower each term, hash long
  terms, and give back the whitespace-joined indexed text plus term pairs.

prepare_prefix_text(terms):
  Truncate each normalized term under the byte budget and give back the joined
  prefix text plus dictionary pairs.

tokenize(text):
  Prepare the text and give back the whitespace-split indexed tokens.

normalize_term(source):
  Stream-safe NFC, lower, then NFC again.

parse_queries(queries):
  Require leaf and byte budgets.
  Parse each query string and give back Expression values.
  Raise QuailRuntimeError on budget or syntax failures.

compile_query(expression, prefixes):
  Compile a Leaf, BooleanExpression, or OrExpression into a Turso FTS query
  string. Expand PREFIX leaves from the prefixes map; empty expansion becomes
  the no-match sentinel. ANY leaves compile to a parenthesized OR of terms.

collect_prefixes(expressions):
  Give back sorted PREFIX leaf strings.

quote_term(value):
  Quote a term for FTS. Raise when quotes or backslashes appear.

_parse / _lex / _lex_phrase / _lex_word:
  Lex terms, phrases, AND, and NOT.
  Reject NUL, bare wildcards, explicit OR, wildcards inside phrases, and
  pure-negative queries.
  Unquoted atoms with hyphens/punctuation tokenize like indexing into an ANY
  leaf (match any term) so AND/NOT binding stays on the whole atom.
  Prefix wildcards still require a single punctuation-free term.
  Charge leaf and phrase budgets.

_Parser:
  parse_or builds implicit OrExpression from adjacent leaves.
  parse_explicit builds BooleanExpression from AND / NOT.
  parse_operand requires a leaf.
