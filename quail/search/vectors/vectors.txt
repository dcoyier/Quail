text_hash(text):
  SHA-256 of UTF-8 text for cache keys.

unit_vector(values):
  Require every component is finite.
  L2-normalize before caching; zero vectors stay zero.
  Raise ValueError when a component or the norm is not finite.

pack_unit_vector(values):
  Little-endian float32 blob for Turso -vector_distance_dot.
