There are import and durable-size bounds:
  MAX_CSV_BYTES is 256 MiB.
  MAX_CSV_ROWS is 200000.
  MAX_DATASET_FIELDS is 1024.
  MAX_DATASET_CELLS is 2000000.
  MAX_DATASET_DURABLE_BYTES is 1024 MiB.
  MAX_SOURCE_FIELD_NAME_BYTES is 512.
  MAX_SOURCE_ENTRY_ID_BYTES is 512.
  MAX_DATASET_SCOPE_ID_BYTES is 512.
  SOURCE_VALUE_DURABLE_ROW_OVERHEAD_BYTES is 256.
  SOURCE_VALUE_DURABLE_ENTRY_KEY_COPIES is 6.
  SOURCE_VALUE_DURABLE_FIELD_KEY_COPIES is 5.
  READ_CHUNK_BYTES is 1 MiB.

estimated_source_value_row_bytes(entry_id, field_name, canonical_value_json):
  Give back overhead plus charged copies of the entry id and field name bytes
  plus the UTF-8 length of the canonical JSON.
