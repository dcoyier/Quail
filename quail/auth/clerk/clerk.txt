AllowlistedPrincipal remembers the resolved TOML user and clerk_user_id.

TokenVerifier.verify(token):
  Give back the Clerk user id (sub).

ClerkJwtVerifier(clerk_domain, authorized_parties):
  Require a non-empty authorized_parties tuple (Clerk application azp/aud
    /client_id party values).
  Derive issuer, JWKS URL, and OAuth userinfo URL from the Clerk domain.
  verify(token): try RS256 JWT (require exp/iat/sub; do not use PyJWT audience
    verification). Require azp, aud, or client_id to intersect
    authorized_parties. On JWT failure, log a claim peek (iss/aud/azp/sub/
    client_id/exp only) and GET /oauth/userinfo with the bearer and read
    sub/user_id (opaque tokens skip the party claim check; Clerk issuer still
    vouches). On userinfo failure, log and raise UnauthorizedError.

StaticTokenVerifier(tokens):
  Test helper mapping exact bearer strings to clerk user ids (no party check).

resolve_allowlisted_user(users, clerk_user_id):
  Give back the matching UserSpec, or raise ForbiddenError.

authenticate_bearer(authorization, verifier, users):
  Require Bearer token.
  Verify and resolve allowlisted user.
  Give back AllowlistedPrincipal.
