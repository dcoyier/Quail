AuthMode is unrestricted or clerk.

DatasetSpec is one CSV dataset declared in the manifest.
DatasetSpec remembers:
  workspace_id
  dataset_id
  source
  name (optional)

WorkspaceSpec is one workspace and its declared datasets.
WorkspaceSpec remembers:
  workspace_id
  datasets

UserSpec is a TOML allowlisted Clerk user.
UserSpec remembers:
  user_id
  clerk_user_id
  workspaces
  default_workspace (optional)
  lock_workspace

QuailConfig is the resolved slim deployment config for quail run.
QuailConfig remembers:
  manifest_path
  database
  feedback
  auth_mode
  bind
  port
  workspace_id (unrestricted only; None for clerk)
  clerk_domain (clerk only; None for unrestricted)
  workspaces
  users
  datasets (flattened for apply)
