configure_execution_slots(max_concurrent_executions):
  Install the process-wide BoundedSemaphore from quail.toml hosting.
  Replace any prior pool.

reset_execution_slots_for_tests:
  Restore the default pool of 2 slots.

configured_execution_slots:
  Give back the configured ceiling (2 until configure is called).

acquire_execution_slot:
  Non-blocking acquire.
  On failure raise QuailServerBusyError with a retry repair_hint.
  Always release in finally.
