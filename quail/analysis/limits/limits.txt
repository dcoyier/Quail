There is a fixed worker memory ceiling of 256 MiB for every time_window.

STANDARD_LIMITS is 30s wall and 15s CPU.
EXTENDED_LIMITS is 100s wall and 60s CPU.

ExecLimits remembers wall_seconds, cpu_seconds, and max_memory_bytes.

validate_time_window(time_window):
  Treat None as standard.
  Require standard or extended.
  Raise QuailSyntaxError when the window is invalid.
  Give back the normalized window name.

limits_for_time_window(time_window):
  Give back the hardcoded ExecLimits for that window.

wall_timeout_error / cpu_timeout_error / memory_limit_error:
  Build QuailRuntimeError (CPU: QuailCpuTimeoutError / cpu_timeout; RSS:
  QuailRssLimitError / rss_limit) with a repair_hint matched to the failure.
  Time timeouts omit the "retry with extended" clause when already extended.
  The RSS message names RSS. The RSS hint mentions len and bytes.

time_repair_hint(already_extended):
  Give back the standard or extended-window timeout repair text.
