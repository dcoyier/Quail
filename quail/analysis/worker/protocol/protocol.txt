ApiCall remembers id, method, args, kwargs, and optional line.

PROTOCOL_VERSION is 1.

encode_value / decode_value:
  Round-trip literals, lists, tuples, dicts, and facade objects
  (Field, Entry, Unit, Operation, Expression, Predicate, GroupExpr, Ranking).

dumps_message / loads_message:
  NDJSON objects with version and sorted compact JSON.
  Failure api_result and execute result payloads may include optional
  repair_hint. PROTOCOL_VERSION stays 1.

_encode_plain:
  Recursively encode sealed operation params (including MappingProxyType nests).
