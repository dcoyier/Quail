group_expr_from_record(record):
  Rebuild a GroupExpr from GroupExpr.to_record() output.
  Bare G0 / G1 records give back the built-ins.
  Otherwise rebuild name, predicate, members, or composition forms.
  Raise QuailRuntimeError when the record is malformed.

entry_from_record(record):
  Rebuild an Entry handle from Entry.to_record() via make_entry.
  Raise when id is missing.

predicate_from_record / expression_from_record / field_from_record /
operation_from_record:
  Rebuild nested symbolic objects from their to_record payloads.
